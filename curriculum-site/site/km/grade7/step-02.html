បង្រៀន — គំនិតគោល

Finish the build: the two sensors underneath and in front, then the mBot2 Shield on top — its battery is built in, so charge it before this lesson — and the CyberPi last. Cables go in with the 10 cm mBuild leads; the guide crosses the 20 cm one out in red, and a cable left loose near a wheel is the commonest cause of a robot that "randomly stops" in week nine.

Then quality control, which is a real engineering practice and not a tidying-up ritual. Each team inspects another team's robot against four points and signs it. You stop seeing your own mistakes after twenty minutes with a screwdriver; somebody else sees them in ten seconds. Everything the class finds today is a fault that does not eat a lesson later.

ប្រយ័ត្ន
  • The ultrasonic sensor must face exactly forward. Sight down it from above before tightening.
  • 10 cm cables for the sensors. Teams reaching for the 20 cm one are on the step the guide crossed out.
  • Left motor into EM1, right into EM2. Swapped means the robot turns when told to go straight, and you will not find out until Step 3.
  • The Shield's battery is built in and charges over USB-C. Start every build day charged.
  • Label each robot with its team name now, while there is still one on the bench that is not labelled.
