បង្រៀន — គំនិតគោល

The mBot2 guide is almost wordless, and that is deliberate: reading an exploded diagram is an engineering skill in itself. Three things to find in every picture before touching a screwdriver — which parts, which way round they go, and which screws. The screw lengths are drawn actual size in the margin; hold yours against the page.

The single most common error in this build is a motor mounted backwards, or the left and right motor cables swapped. Neither shows up until the robot drives — and then it turns when you told it to go straight. So there is a rule: no wheels go on until another team has checked the orientation.
មគ្គុទ្ទេសក៍ចាប់ផ្ដើមរហ័ស — មេរៀននេះ
Guidembot2.pdf pp. 9–12, steps ①–③
Firstmotor cables in BEFORE the motor goes in
ScrewsM4×8 for motors · M2.5×12 for wheels
ប្រយ័ត្ន
  • Plug each motor cable in BEFORE the motor goes into the chassis. The port is unreachable afterwards, and every year a team takes a motor back out to discover this.
  • The four long M4×25 screws belong to the next step. A team reaching for them today is on the wrong page.
  • Snug, not tight. These screws go into plastic and they will strip.
  • Half-built is fine. Label the tray with the team name and put it on the shelf.

10′Open the box over a tray and sort the screws by size. This is the single biggest time-saver of the day, and the teams who skip it will be hunting for an M4×8 in twenty minutes.

40′Build guide steps ①–③: motor cables in first, motors into the chassis with M4×8, wheel hubs pressed into the tyres, wheels onto the shafts with M2.5×12.

◉ លទ្ធផលរំពឹងទុកនៅចុងបញ្ចប់មេរៀននេះ
👁WATCH FOR

👁WATCH FOR

👁WATCH FOR
