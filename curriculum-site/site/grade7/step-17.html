Teach it — the concept

A buffer hour is available here and is deliberately not in the timetable above — add it only if the class is not level. The stated hours are what the course takes when nobody needs catching up. If the class is level, spend it on the extension: coaches build a robot that records its route, finds the first left turn in it, and replays only the part after that.

Stage D is about naming and organising what the robot already does, and half of what it organises is memory. A student who cannot yet make a counter behave will not enjoy wrapping one in a function.

Same rules as before: hands-on, coaches keep their hands off other people's robots, buffer hour goes wherever the class needs it.

Watch out
  • Challenge 1 separates understanding from copying. If their counter jumps by twenty they have not passed, however good the rest looks.
  • Challenge 3 is the one that proves they understand length-of rather than having memorised a number.
  • Rotate the coaching pairs from Checkpoint 2 — different pairings surface different gaps.

Lesson flow — minute by minute

10′Set up three stations. Remind everyone the gate holds.
✓Three stations ready, and a class that knows the gate holds until everyone is through.
30′The three challenges: (1) count something the robot has no built-in block for, and show it labelled on screen — counting once per event, not once per loop. (2) record at least five moves into a list and replay them. (3) remove the last item from a list without knowing how long the list is.
✓Three challenges demonstrated: a labelled count of something with no built-in block, five moves recorded and replayed, and the last item removed without knowing the list length.
20′Pairing: everyone through all three coaches someone who is not.
✓Everyone through all three is coaching someone who is not.
◉ Expected result at the end of this session
Teacher check
  • Every student demonstrated all three challenges
  • Every counter counts once per event, not once per loop
  • The class starts Stage D together
