Teach it — the concept

Build in the order of your diagram and test each arrow before adding the next. The temptation is to build it all and then debug it all, and it never works — when three things are new at once you cannot tell which one is broken.

The one genuinely new piece is getting round the obstacle and finding the line again. Back up, arc around it, then creep forward until a probe sees the line and go back to following. A timed arc is fine; tune it with the obstacle in two different places, because an arc that works in one spot has been tuned to a route rather than written as a behaviour.
Quick Start Guide — this session

Newback up → arc → creep until line

Floorthe full course: line, obstacle, red zone
Watch out
  • Keep the obstacle off the course while phases 1 and 4 are being built. One new variable at a time, or every bug is ambiguous.
  • The arc must clear the obstacle but land near the line. That is pure tuning and it is supposed to take a while.
  • Log every attempt. Under pressure memory lies and paper does not — "we fail at the zone three times in four" is a work item, "it keeps breaking" is a mood.
