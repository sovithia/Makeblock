Teach it — the concept

Take last year's robot apart properly. Teardown is a skill, not destruction: a part only leaves the robot if it lands in the right labelled tray. Reading the build guide backwards is the same diagram literacy as reading it forwards, and next week you will be glad the screws are sorted.

Then the one error that costs a whole session if it survives: the two track modules mirror each other. Building two identical ones feels right and is wrong, and it is not obvious until the Rover drives in a circle. Check each module against the guide page before you finish it — and get another team to check it too, because you stop seeing your own work after twenty minutes.

Watch out
  • The Rover guide is the printed booklet in the add-on box, front to back. There is no digital version — the pages above are photographs of it. Build from the booklet in your hands; the photographs only show where you are in it. Do not use mbot2_addons.pdf: despite its cover title that file is the Ranger guide, a different robot, with the motor cables straight instead of crossed and the servos on S3/S4 instead of S1.
  • The mirror error is the classic one and it is nearly invisible until the robot drives. Insist on the cross-check — a builder cannot see their own mistake by this point.
  • After tightening each hub screw, back the nut off 90° counter-clockwise. The wheel must spin freely or the motors strain all year.
  • Track grain direction matters; both tracks point the same way. There is a magnified inset in the guide showing it.

Lesson flow — minute by minute

15′Quick recap in teams: name five things last year's robot could do. Keep the list — Step 2 asks which of them still work.
✓A written list of five things last year’s robot could do, kept for Step 2.
20′Structured teardown into labelled trays. Electronics padded and separate. Photograph the full trays.
✓Every part in a labelled tray, electronics padded and apart, and a photograph of the full trays.
45′Build both track-wheel modules from the guide. Check each against the page before you finish it, not after.
✓Two track-wheel modules built, each checked against the page before it was finished.
20′Cross-check: another team holds your two modules side by side against the guide and signs that they mirror.
✓Another team has held your two modules side by side against the guide and signed that they mirror.
15′Free-spin and tension test on both. Back the nut off 90° and feel the difference between bound and free.
✓Both modules spin freely, and you can feel the difference between a bound nut and a free one.
5′Trays back in order. Log which parts you could not name.
✓Trays back in order, and a log line naming the parts you could not name.
